CHALLENGE CONTEXT

The Problem: Synthetic Gaze in XR Headsets

Next-generation XR headsets (Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest, Samsung XR) rely critically on real-time eye tracking for:

  • Foveated Rendering: Concentrating GPU power on where the user looks, saving 40-70% shading power.
  • Spatial Interaction: Intuitive gaze-and-pinch interaction.
  • Avatar Realism: Expressive digital avatars in VR/MR.

The Core Bottleneck: Training computer vision gaze models requires large datasets with millimeter-accurate ground truth. Most simulations merely translate pupil textures in 2D, introducing severe perspective and parallax distortion.

Official Challenge Requirements (Track 01)

  • True 3D Eyeball Rotation around physical centers (NO 2D translation).
  • Two Controllable Bones: Eye_L and Eye_R.
  • Calibrated Binocular Convergence: Target at 35 cm forward from eye midpoint.
  • Fixed Virtual Camera: 40 cm forward, 1024 × 1024 PNG.
  • Automated Suite: 5-direction validation, 10 clockwise renders, metadata.json.
MATHEMATICAL CALIBRATION

Binocular Convergence & Camera Calibration

Fixation Target Geometry (35 cm)

At fixation distance $D = 0.35\,\text{m}$, voluntary oculomotor ranges $\theta_{H,\max} = 25^\circ$, $\theta_{V,\max} = 20^\circ$:

X_target = 0.35 * tan(h * 25°)
Z_target = 0.35 * tan(v * 20°)
Target = (X_target, -0.35, Z_target)

At center rest ($h=0, v=0$), inward convergence angle is:
$$\alpha = \arctan(0.032 / 0.35) = 5.23^\circ \text{ inward}$$

Camera Calibration ($K$) (40 cm)

Fixed pinhole camera positioned at $(0.0, -0.40, 0.0)\,\text{m}$ facing $+Y$:

f = 50.0 mm | Sensor = 36.0 x 36.0 mm
fx = fy = 50 * (1024 / 36) = 1422.2222 px
cx = cy = 512.0 px

[1422.2222, 0.0000, 512.0],

GAZE SEQUENCE

Ten Clockwise Gaze Renders & Metadata

Sampled continuously across a circular trajectory:

theta_k = 2 * pi * k / 10
h = sin(theta_k)
v = cos(theta_k) for k = 0, ..., 9
